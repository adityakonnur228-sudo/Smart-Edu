# VTU 2022 Scheme — Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AIML) Knowledge Base

This knowledge base repository contains the official, verified curriculum and syllabus catalog for the Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU) 2022 Scheme for **B.E. in Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning)**.

## Curriculum Catalog Overview

- **University:** Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi
- **Scheme:** 2022 Scheme (CBCS / Outcome Based Education)
- **Programme:** Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.)
- **Branch:** Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AIML)
- **Total Requested Subject Categories:** 15
- **Officially Verified Courses in Scheme:** 13 (covering Core, Course Options, Professional Electives, and Enhancement Courses)
- **Supplementary / Unverified Courses:** 2 (Robotic Process Automation, Computer Graphics — not part of 2022 AIML Scheme)
- **Combined Courses:** Software Engineering & Project Management (`BCS501`, Semester V)
