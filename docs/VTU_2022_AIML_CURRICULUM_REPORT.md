## 1. Overview of the Official 2022 AIML Scheme

The official Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU) 2022 Scheme of Teaching and Examinations for Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) in **Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning)** was established by the Joint Board of Studies (JBOS 10.02.2023 / V5) and made effective starting from the academic year 2023-24.

Key characteristics of the 2022 AIML curriculum include:
- **Total Scheme Duration:** 8 Semesters (Semesters 3 to 8 detailed across 14 pages of the official scheme document).
- **Credit Framework:** 160 total credits across 4 years.
- **Course Classifications:**
  - **IPCC (Integrated Professional Core Course):** Theory integrated with laboratory practicals (4 credits, 3:0:2, 100 total marks split 50 CIE / 50 SEE).
  - **PCC (Professional Core Course):** Core theory courses (3 or 4 credits, 3:0:0 or 4:0:0).
  - **PCCL (Professional Core Course Laboratory):** Standalone laboratory practicals (1 credit, 0:0:2).
  - **ESC/ETC/PLC (Engineering Science / Emerging Technology / Programming Language Course):** Language & foundational course options (3 credits, 2:0:2).
  - **PEC (Professional Elective Course):** Specialized domain electives (3 credits, 3:0:0).
  - **AEC / SEC (Ability Enhancement Course / Skill Enhancement Course):** Practical application skills (1 credit, 0:0:1:0 or 0:0:2).
  - **OEC (Open Elective Course):** Inter-departmental open electives offered across colleges.

## 3. Important Academic Distinctions & Curriculum Structure

1. **Core Courses:**
   - `BCS303 (Operating Systems)`, `BCS304 (Data Structures and Applications)`, `BCS401 (Analysis & Design of Algorithms)`, `BCS403 (Database Management Systems)`, `BCS501 (Software Engineering & Project Management)`, `BCS502 (Computer Networks)`, and `BCS703 (Cryptography & Network Security)` are official mandatory Professional Core Courses required for graduation.
2. **Programming Language Course Options (Java vs. Python):**
   - In Semester III, students select one Programming Language Course (PLC) from a prescribed bucket:
     - `BCS306A`: Object Oriented Programming with Java (2:0:2, 3 credits)
     - `BDS306B`: Python Programming for Data Science (2:0:2, 3 credits)
     - `BDS306C`: Data Analytics with R
   - Both `BCS306A` and `BDS306B` are fully extracted into the catalog with their 5 modules and dedicated laboratory components.
3. **Combined BCS501 Course (Software Engineering & Project Management):**
   - In Semester V, VTU explicitly combines Software Engineering and Project Management into a single 3-credit professional core course under code `BCS501`.
   - **Modules 1, 2, and 3** cover traditional and agile software engineering (Nature of software, process models, requirements engineering, UML, XP, Scrum, DSDM).
   - **Modules 4 and 5** cover software project planning, contract management, cost-benefit evaluation (NPV, ROI), risk evaluation, ISO 9126 quality metrics, and CMMI process capability.
   - The portal catalog maps both category requests (`software_engineering` and `project_management`) to the identical verified `BCS501` curriculum structure without creating duplicate or artificial courses.
4. **Professional Electives (Parallel Computing & Cloud Computing):**
   - `BCI714B (Parallel Computing)` is an official 7th-semester Professional Elective (PEC-3) offering in-depth coverage of OpenMP, MPI, and Nvidia CUDA GPU programming.
   - `BIS613D (Cloud Computing & Security)` is an official 6th-semester Professional Elective (PEC-2) covering NIST cloud models, IaaS/PaaS/SaaS architectures, hypervisors/virtualization, and IAM/cloud security boundaries.
5. **Generative AI as an Ability Enhancement Course:**
   - Under code `BAIL657C`, Generative AI is officially classified as an Ability Enhancement Course / Skill Enhancement Course-V (AEC/SEC) in Semester VI (1 credit, practical format).
   - It prescribes 10 hands-on laboratory experiments spanning word embeddings (Word2Vec, GloVe), vector arithmetic, PCA/t-SNE visualization, Hugging Face transformers, LangChain, Cohere API, and building an Indian Penal Code (IPC) legal RAG chatbot.
6. **Supplementary & Unverified Categories (RPA & Computer Graphics):**
   - **Robotic Process Automation (RPA):** Thorough search of all 2022 scheme matrices and syllabus PDFs confirmed that RPA is not offered as an official subject for the 2022 AIML branch. It is categorized as `SUPPLEMENTARY_UNVERIFIED`.
   - **Computer Graphics:** Computer Graphics was previously taught in the 2018 CSE scheme (under course code `18CS62`), but was eliminated from the 2022 AIML core curriculum to make room for specialized machine learning, deep learning, and data science subjects. It is categorized as `SUPPLEMENTARY_UNVERIFIED`.
